Our Facilities

We offer healthcare services to every client.

We handle emergency cases with utmost priority. Our reputation is founded on our ability to deliver first-class services comparable in standard to those obtained anywhere in the world. You can trust us to handle all cases successfully.

Your testing will be performed by highly trained, nationally certified staff using technically advanced equipment. Most results are available the same day or next day. We perform 95% of all ordered tests at our own laboratory.

The Patient Relations Department helps visitors navigate medical encounters and gives patients an opportunity to report specific concerns. Our Patient Relations staff are available 24/7 to assist you.
